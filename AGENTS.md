# Agent Instructions

1. If the interview target is unclear, ask clarifying questions before researching or drafting.
2. Browse for current company, role, financial, news, and interviewer facts unless the user explicitly says not to.
3. Use only public, professional, interview-relevant information for interviewer research.
4. Label uncertain claims and separate facts from inference.
5. Keep generated briefs practical: what to remember, what to say, what to ask, and what risks to prepare for.
