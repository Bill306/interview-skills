# Hiring Manager Round Example

Prompt:

```text
Use $interview-skills to prepare me for a hiring manager round at a public fintech company. The role is strategic finance manager.
```

Expected shape:

1. Company and business model briefing
2. Recent financial and operating trends
3. What the hiring manager likely cares about
4. Role success profile
5. Candidate stories to prepare
6. Risks or gaps to address
7. Tailored questions on priorities, metrics, and team cadence
8. Sources with evidence quality labels
