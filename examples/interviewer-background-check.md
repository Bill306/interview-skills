# Interviewer Background Check Example

Prompt:

```text
Use $interview-skills to prepare for an interview with Jane Doe, Head of Product at ExampleCo.
```

Expected shape:

1. Public professional profile
2. Current scope and likely interview angle
3. Relevant public work, talks, writing, or product launches
4. What this person may care about in the role
5. Interviewer-specific questions
6. Privacy-safe source notes
