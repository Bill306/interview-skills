# Privacy and Source Standards

## Interviewer Research Boundaries

Use only public, professional, interview-relevant information:

- Current role
- Public career history
- Public writing, talks, interviews, patents, publications, repositories, or portfolio work
- Professional interests that connect to the role or company

Do not collect or infer:

- Home address or private contact details
- Family details
- Protected traits
- Medical, financial, political, religious, or intimate personal information
- Non-professional speculation

## Source Preference

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Official company, investor, regulatory, or public profile sources
2. Reputable press and trade publications
3. Public talks, podcasts, interviews, blogs, papers, or repositories
4. Review sites, forums, and social media only as anecdotal signals

## Current Information

Always browse for current facts unless the user explicitly says not to. News, financials, job postings, and interviewer roles can change quickly.
