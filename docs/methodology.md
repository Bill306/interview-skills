# Methodology

Interview preparation should turn research into usable interview strategy.

## Research Layers

1. Company reality
   - What the company sells
   - Who buys it
   - How the business makes money
   - What has changed recently

2. Role reality
   - What the job description says
   - Why the company may need the role now
   - Which outcomes the role likely owns
   - What evidence the candidate should show

3. Interviewer reality
   - What the interviewer publicly works on
   - How their scope relates to the role
   - What they may probe
   - Which questions would be respectful and specific

## Depth Levels

Quick screen:

- 5-10 bullets
- Company basics
- One "why this company" angle
- Three questions to ask

Standard prep:

- Full brief
- Recent news
- Role analysis
- Candidate positioning
- Interviewer context if known

Deep dive:

- Financial or operating trend analysis
- Competitive landscape
- Culture and leadership themes
- Interview scenario planning
- Multiple tailored question sets

## Evidence Handling

Never flatten all evidence into the same confidence level. Label claims as confirmed, reported, anecdotal, or inferred. For time-sensitive facts, include dates and links.
